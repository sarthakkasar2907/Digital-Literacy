Do for responsible social media use as a college student:-
•Regularly check who can see your posts and untag yourself from "messy" photos
•See what a recruiter sees. If the first result is an old, embarrassing account, try to deactivate it.
•Follow leaders in your field of study and engage with their content meaningfully.
•Post about that Dean’s List mention, a volunteer event, or a project you’re proud of.
•Even on non-LinkedIn platforms, having one clean, recognizable photo helps your personal brand.
Don't for responsible social media use as a college student:-
•If you're angry at a professor or a roommate, put the phone down.
•Posting "live" stories of exactly where you are can be a safety risk. Wait until you've left the venue.
•Getting into a public "comment war" makes you look unprofessional, regardless of who started it.
•This includes underage drinking or anything that violates your University’s Code of Conduct.
•Even if your boss is difficult, venting online is a "red flag" for future employers.
